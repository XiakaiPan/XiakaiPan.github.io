# Xiakai Pan (潘夏凯)

**AI Computing & Task Intelligence**

*Structural modeling from execution substrates to evolving task substrates.*

I work on computer architecture, AI infrastructure, compilers and runtime systems. My long-term research interests connect three complementary modeling paradigms with two parallel research series.

## Research Program

### Three Models — World Modeling Methodology

**Formal · Simulation · Statistical** — different but complementary contracts for representing legal structure, predicting state transitions, and learning from data.

### SSG — System Substrate Graph

**Modeling the AI computing world.** A Workload–System–Schedule representation across operator, training/inference/RL, and agent execution. Current work includes functional execution prototypes, cross-level composition, and structure-preserving simulation.

[Explore SSG research](/research/#series-a--system-substrate-graph-ssg)

### TSG — Task Substrate Graph

**Modeling the human–AI task world.** Persistent tasks organized by Actor, Spec and Impl, with attention to task instantiation, evolution and cross-harness collaboration.

[Explore TSG research](/research/#series-b--task-substrate-graph-tsg)
